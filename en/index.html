Services

Your home, cared for while you are away

For second home owners and families who are away for long periods.

Regular home checks

We visit your home as often as you choose and make sure everything is in order.

  • Airing the rooms
  • Damp, leaks and drains
  • Electricity, fridge and taps
  • Post and leaflets

Ready for your arrival

Before you arrive, we air the house, do a light clean and stock the fridge from your shopping list.

  • Groceries at receipt price, no mark-up
  • Closing up the house when you leave

Plants and delicate items

We water and look after your plants and keep an eye on anything that suffers during a long absence.

Key holding

We keep your keys and let in tradespeople, deliveries or guests whenever you need.

Photo reports

After every visit you get a short report with photos by WhatsApp or email.

Repair coordination

If we spot a problem, we let you know and arrange the repair with trusted local professionals.

What we don't do: we don't carry out repairs ourselves (we coordinate them with outside professionals, paid for by the owner) and we don't look after pets.

Simple and worry-free

01

FAQ

Your questions answered

What happens if you find a problem at the house?

We tell you straight away, with photos, and if you wish we arrange the repair with a local professional. You see the quote first, and we are there to let them in and keep an eye on the work.

How do you keep my keys?

Keys are labelled with a code, never with your address, and are only used for the visits and services you authorise.

How does the shopping before my arrival work?

Send us your list and we stock the fridge and cupboards. We only charge what's on the receipt, with no mark-up.

Can I pause my plan while I'm staying at the house?

Yes. If you spend the summer or a long stay at your home, we pause your plan for those months so you don't pay for visits you don't need.

Do you look after pets?

No, our service focuses on the home: checks, airing, light cleaning, plants and getting everything ready for your arrival.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. You can cancel whenever you like. If you pay for the year up front, you get 2 months free.
